How Connecting Works

Tapping Connect WhatsApp opens the Meta/WhatsApp Business signup flow directly in the app. You'll log in with your Facebook account, select or create a WhatsApp Business Account, and choose the phone number to connect.

Mycroshop will then act as your AI agent on that number — responding to customers automatically on your behalf.
